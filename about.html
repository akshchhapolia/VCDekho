Democratizing
Funding for Indian Founders

Fundraising shouldn't be about who you know. We are building the infrastructure to make venture capital discovery, research, and outreach transparent, efficient, and accessible to every startup builder in India.

Our Mission

Every year, thousands of brilliant startup ideas in India stall or fade away simply because founders can't connect with the right investors. Traditional fundraising relies heavily on warm introductions, gatekeepers, and weeks of manual spreadsheet research.

VC Dekho is changing that. We catalog active Indian venture funds, angel syndicates, and micro-VCs. We break down their investment thesis, sector focus, check sizes, and contact endpoints so you can build highly targeted outreach lists in minutes.

Whether you are in the Idea Stage, building an MVP, or scaling early growth, VC Dekho gives you the intelligence to fundraise smarter and raise faster.
